## Cause

No one-program template for either K at M=32 N=128.

K=512 is the native matvec reduction. `rows > 1` and no `(32,512,128)` parity key, so the planner emits M=1: 32 programs. Same class as the encoder 1875-path (per-row native), without the K-split or bias adds. Keep threshold was `rc=0` and `programs<=4`. 32 fails it. Package not kept.

K=1024 would split each row into two K=512 matvecs that both write the padded y surface. Without a K-accum add, those writes overlap. Fail-fast `h13.unsupported-chain`, 0 programs. Not the 1875 package.

Do not compose `(32,256,128)` over K. Do not pad N to steal another template.
