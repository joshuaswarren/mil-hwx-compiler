# Generic Program Coalescing Baseline

## Build and host

- Compiler commit: `7fc6c42d1f5560ada24f74e6a193f7e70a342abe`
- Host: Mac16,10 with M4
- OS build: 25D125
- ANECompiler: 9.202.0
- Data type: FP16
- Timing protocol: 20 warmups, then two independent runs. Each run used five alternating batches of 2,000 evaluations per compiler and workload, for 10,000 timed evaluations per result.

## Warm latency

| Workload | Shape | Apple | Research | Ratio | Research artifacts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Flash attention 2 | sequence 128, head dimension 128 | 132.21 us | 1113.44 us | 8.42x | 8 |
| Matmul and GELU | output width 256 | 105.64 us | 225.04 us | 2.13x | 2 |

The outputs matched element for element: 16,384 values for flash attention and 65,536 values for matmul-GELU.

The exact affine-scan graph was rejected by Apple's compiler as `InvalidMILProgram`, so it has no Apple latency baseline.

## Runtime profile

A symbolized five-second sample was taken during a long research-runtime batch.

| Workload | All samples | In research evaluate method | In Apple direct evaluate | In Mach message wait | Research preparation above Apple call |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Flash attention 2 | 3757 | 3654 | 3636 | 3457 | 18, 0.48% |
| Matmul and GELU | 3867 | 3784 | 3774 | 3685 | 10, 0.26% |

The dominant cost is the synchronous ANE submission path. Binding sorting, IOSurface wrapping, and request construction account for less than 0.5 percent of samples. Request-object caching was rejected as the first optimization.

## Decoded HWX structure

No Apple-generated bytes are stored in the repository. The table records marker counts from read-only parsing.

| Program | HWX files | DMA load markers | DMA intermediate markers | DMA store markers | Task endings | Tile starts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Apple flash attention 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 3 | 2 |
| Research flash attention 2 | 8 | varies by file | 2 total | 8 total | 11 total | 2 total |
| Apple matmul and GELU | 1 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 1 |
| Research matmul and GELU | 2 | 3 total | 1 total | 2 total | 3 total | 1 total |

Apple's output is a fused program, not a concatenation of the research compiler's standalone files. The optimization must remove unnecessary external intermediates and form valid combined task programs before runtime dispatch.
